All About Birds

Birds are high energy, warm-blooded vertebrates that are surprisingly the only living animals with feathers. Birds evolved from the same lineage as ancient theropod dinosaurs, modern birds have developed incredible adaptations for flight, such as hollow bones to keep them light and powerful chest muscles. Their beaks come in all sorts of shapes and sizes, designed for the food they eat, whether it's cracking seeds, sipping nectar, or hunting prey. From the colorful macaws in the rainforest to the flightless penguins in the Antarctic, birds are found in every climate and continent on the planet.

GROUP NAME: Birds

SCIENTIFIC CLASS: Aves

TYPE: Warm-blooded Vertebrates

BODY COVERING: Feathers

REPRODUCTION: Lay eggs

ABILITIES: Flight (most species)

HABITAT: Worldwide (land, air, and some water)
