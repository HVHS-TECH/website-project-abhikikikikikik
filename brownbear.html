All About the Brown Bear

Brown bears are well known for their long winter hibernations, often retreating to dens in caves or hollow logs as the temperature drops. During this time, the brown bear's heart rate slows down significantly and their body temperature gradually decreases to save energy. Unlike a few other hibernators, bears are "light" sleepers and often wake up quickly if disturbed. They spend all their time during summer and autumn eating as much as possible to build up a thick layer of fat, providing the nutrients and water they need to survive until spring.

COMMON NAME: Brown Bear

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Ursus arctos

TYPE: Mammal

DIET: Omnivore (plants, fish, small animals)

HABITAT: Forests, mountains, and tundra

LIFE SPAN: 20–30 years

SPECIAL TRAIT: Powerful build hibernation
