All About Arachnids

Arachnids—which include spiders, scorpions, and ticks—are often mistaken for insects but are a completely separate class. The easiest way to tell them apart is by counting their legs, since arachnids always always have eight. They also don't have antennae and wings. Many arachnids are great hunters, spiders are famous for weaving intricate silk webs to catch prey, while scorpions use venomous stingers for protection. They have amazing survival instincts and can be found in almost every habitat, from dark caves to dry deserts.

GROUP NAME: Arachnids

SCIENTIFIC CLASS: Arachnida

TYPE: Invertebrates

INCLUDES: Spiders, Scorpions, Ticks, Mites

BODY STRUCTURE: 2 main parts (cephalothorax abdomen)

LEGS: 8 legs

HABITAT: Land environments worldwide
