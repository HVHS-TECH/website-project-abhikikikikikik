All About Arachnids

Arachnids include spiders, scorpions. They are often mistaken for insects but are a completely different. The easiest way to tell them apart from insects is by counting their legs, since arachnids always have eight legs. Arachnids don't have antennae and wings. Many arachnids are great hunters, spiders are famous for weaving silk webs to catch prey, while scorpions use venomous stingers for protection. Arachnids have amazing survival instincts and can be found in almost every habitat, from dark caves to dry deserts.

GROUP NAME: Arachnids

SCIENTIFIC CLASS: Arachnida

TYPE: Invertebrates

INCLUDES: Spiders, Scorpions, Ticks, Mites

BODY STRUCTURE: 2 main parts (cephalothorax abdomen)

LEGS: 8 legs

HABITAT: Land environments worldwide
