All About Theropods

Theropods are a famous clade of dinosaurs and are recognized by their hollow bones and three-toed feet. When people think about dinosaurs, the Tyrannosaurus Rex usually comes to mind, but this group is actually a lot more diverse than it looks. Scientific evidence proves that modern birds are actually living theropods! So whenever you see a sparrow or a chicken, you are looking at a tiny, feathered descendant of some of the most fearsome hunters likes the T-Rex.

GROUP NAME: Theropods

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Theropoda ("Beast-footed")

DIET: Primarily Carnivorous

LIMBS: Bipedal (walks on two legs)

FAMOUS MEMBERS: T-Rex, Velociraptor, Spinosaurus

MODERN DESCENDANTS: All modern-day birds

KEY FEATURE: Hollow bones and three-toed limbs
