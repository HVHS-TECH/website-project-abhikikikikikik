All About Theropods

Theropods are a famous clade of dinosaurs characterized by their hollow bones and three-toed limbs. When people think about notorious dinosaurs, the Tyrannosaurus Rex usually comes to mind, but this group is actually much more diverse than it looks. In fact, scientific evidence proves that modern birds are actually living theropods! So every time you see a sparrow or a chicken, you are looking at a tiny, feathered descendant of the same lineage that once included some of the most fearsome hunters likes the T-Rex.

GROUP NAME: Theropods

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Theropoda ("Beast-footed")

DIET: Primarily Carnivorous

LIMBS: Bipedal (walks on two legs)

FAMOUS MEMBERS: T-Rex, Velociraptor, Spinosaurus

MODERN DESCENDANTS: All modern-day birds

KEY FEATURE: Hollow bones and three-toed limbs
