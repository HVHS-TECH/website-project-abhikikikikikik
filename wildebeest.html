All About Wildebeest

The Wildebeest migration, often called "The Great Migration," by researchers is one of the most spectacular wildlife events on the planet. Over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles travel in a giant circle formation through the Serengeti in Tanzania and the Maasai Mara in Kenya. These herds often face dangerous river crossings filled with crocodiles and must stay alert for the constant threats from predators in the savanna.

SPECIES: Blue Wildebeest

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Connochaetes taurinus

MIGRATION GROUP: Over 1.5 million individuals

DISTANCE: 800 km circular journey annually

DIET: Herbivore (Short green grass)

HABITAT: African Savanna (Serengeti and Masai Mara)

KEY FEATURE: "The Great Migration" across crocodile-filled rivers
