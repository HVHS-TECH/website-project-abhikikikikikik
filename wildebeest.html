All About Wildebeest

The Wildebeest migration, often called "The Great Migration," is one of the most spectacular wildlife events on the planet. Over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles travel in a giant circle formation through the Serengeti in Tanzania and the Maasai Mara in Kenya. Motivated to find fresh grass and water, these animals often face dangerous river crossings filled with crocodiles and constant threats from predators on the savanna, demonstrating the true power of survival and determination.

SPECIES: Blue Wildebeest

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Connochaetes taurinus

MIGRATION GROUP: Over 1.5 million individuals

DISTANCE: 800 km circular journey annually

DIET: Herbivore (Short green grass)

HABITAT: African Savanna (Serengeti and Masai Mara)

KEY FEATURE: "The Great Migration" across crocodile-filled rivers
