All About Reptiles

Reptiles are a widely diverse group of cold-blooded vertebrates that are easily recognized by their scaly skin. Unlike amphibians, reptiles lay eggs on land, which allowed them to move away from the water and situate in almost every environment on Earth. From the massive Komodo dragon to the worm snake, all reptiles are "ectothermic," meaning they rely on the sun and their surroundings to regulate their body temperature. Without the sun's heat, they would die due to not being able to sustain a steady body temperature. This is why you’d often see them basking on warm rocks during the day!

GROUP NAME: Reptiles

SCIENTIFIC CLASS: Reptilia

BLOOD TYPE: Ectothermic (Cold-blooded)

SKIN TYPE: Dry scales or scutes

INCLUDES: Snakes, Lizards, Turtles, Crocodiles

HABITAT: Every continent except Antarctica

KEY FEATURE: Laying sturdy, leathery eggs on land
