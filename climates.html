Global Climates

Climate is a long term pattern of weather in a specific area, and it is the most important factor in determining where animals can live. From the scorching heat of the Sahara Desert to the frozen tundra of the Arctic, every ecosystem is shaped by its temperature, rainfall, and sunlight. Animals must develop incredible physical and behavioral traits just to survive the unique challenges of their local environment.

Adapting to the Elements

Whether an animal lives in a Tropical Rainforest, a Temperate Forest, or a Dry Desert, its entire life cycle is tied to the climate. Some animals have thick blubber to withstand the cold, while others have large ears to radiate heat in the tropics. Understanding these climate zones helps us see how life has branched out and specialized to fit every niche on our beautiful, diverse planet!
