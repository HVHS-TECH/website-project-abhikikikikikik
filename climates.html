Global Climates

Climate is a long term pattern of weather in a specific area, and it is the most important factor in determining where animals can live. From scorching hot areas like the Sahara Desert to the freezing land of the Arctic, every ecosystem is shaped by its temperature, rainfall, and sunlight. In order for animals to live in these areas, they must develop physical and behavioral traits just to survive the unique challenges of their local environment.

Adapting to the Elements

Whether an animal lives in a Tropical Rainforest, a Freezing Tundra, or a Dry Desert, its entire life cycle is tied to the climate. Some animals have thick blubber to live with the cold, while some have large ears to help release heat in the tropics. Understanding these climate zones helps us see how life has branched out and specialized to fit every type of climate type there is.
