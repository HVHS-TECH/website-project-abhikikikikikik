All About Amphibians

Amphibians, such as frogs, toads, and salamanders, are unique because they lead a "double life." Most amphibians begin their journey in the water as larvae, generally breathing through gills. As they grow, they go through a transformation called "metamorphosis" to develop lungs in order to live and survive on land. Amphibians have thin, moist skin that actually helps them breathe, but it also means they must stay near damp environments to keep themselves from drying out.

GROUP NAME: Amphibians

SCIENTIFIC CLASS: Amphibia

TYPE: Cold-blooded Vertebrates

INCLUDES: Frogs, Toads, Salamanders, Newts

HABITAT: Freshwater Damp Environments

SKIN: Moist and Permeable

LIFE CYCLE: Metamorphosis (water land)
