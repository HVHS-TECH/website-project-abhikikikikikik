All About Desert Climates

Desert climates are known for their extreme lack of water, often receiving very little rainfall throughout the entire year. Though many people think of deserts as just empty, sandy, and hot, they are actually full of life. Temperatures vary and can be boiling hot during the day and freezing cold at night, forcing animals who live in such climates to adopt amazing survival instincts. Many desert creatures are nocturnal, staying underground during the heat of the day to save moisture, while others have evolved to go long periods without ever taking a drink of water.

REGION TYPE: Desert Climate

AVERAGE RAINFALL: Less than 25cm per year

TEMPERATURE: Extreme (Hot days ; Cold nights)

VEGETATION: Cacti, Succulents, Scrub brush

NATIVE ANIMALS: Camels, Fennec Foxes, Scorpions

KEY FEATURE: Arid landscape and high evaporation

LOCATIONS: Sahara, Gobi, Arabian, Outback
