All About the Platypus

The Platypus is easily one of the strangest and most fascinating creatures in the animal kingdom. Found in the freshwater areas of Australia, it looks like a mashup of different animals: it has a bill like a duck, a tail like a beaver, and feet like an otter. Even more surprising is that it is a monotreme, a rare type of mammal that lays leathery eggs instead of giving birth to live young. They are also one of the few venomous mammals in the world, as males have sharp spurs on their hind legs that can deliver a painful sting.

SPECIES: Platypus

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Ornithorhynchus anatinus

CLASSIFICATION: Monotreme (Egg-laying mammal)

HABITAT: Rivers and streams of Eastern Australia

SENSORY: Electroreception (detects prey with its bill)

DEFENSE: Males have venomous spurs on their hind feet

KEY FEATURE: Duck-like bill, beaver-like tail, and otter-like feet
