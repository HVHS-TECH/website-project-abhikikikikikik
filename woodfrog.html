All About Woodfrogs

The Wood Frog has one of the most incredible survival strategies in the world. When winter comes around, these frogs allow their entire bodies to freeze solid! Their breathing stops and their hearts shut down completely, making them appear dead. Wood frogs produce a "antifreeze" solution in their blood that prevents ice crystals from damaging their cells. When the winter season is finally over, the frog miraculously "comes back to life" and practices the same hibernation techniques when winter approaches again.

SPECIES: Wood Frog

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Lithobates sylvaticus

HIBERNATION TYPE: Cryoprotection (Freezing)

SURVIVAL TRICK: 65% of their body water turns to ice

HEART RATE: Completely stops during freezing

HABITAT: Arctic Circle and North American forests

KEY FEATURE: Natural "antifreeze" in their blood prevents cell damage
