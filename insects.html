All About Insects

Insects are the most numerous group of animals on the planet, making up over half of all living organisms. Every insect has a body divided into three clear parts: the head, thorax, and abdomen, along with six legs and a pair of antennae. Many insects like butterflies and bees also have wings that allow them to travel long distances. From pollinating our flowers to helping break down organic waste, insects are essential to almost every ecosystem on Earth.

GROUP NAME: Insects

SCIENTIFIC CLASS: Insecta

BODY PARTS: Head, Thorax, and Abdomen

LEGS: 6 Jointed Legs

INCLUDES: Ants, Bees, Butterflies, Beetles

SENSORY: One pair of antennae

KEY FACT: They make up more than half of all living organisms on Earth
