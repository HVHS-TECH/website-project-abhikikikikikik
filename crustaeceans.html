All About Crustaceans

Crustaceans are a diverse group of invertebrates that mostly live in the water, including animals like crabs, lobsters, and shrimp. They are famous for their hard exoskeletons that acts like a suit of armor to protect them from potential predators. Since the shell doesn't actually grow with them, they have to shed it to grow a bigger one. Most crustaceans also have two pairs of antennae and specialized limbs that they can use for swimming, crawling, or even cracking open tough shells.

GROUP NAME: Crustaceans

SCIENTIFIC SUBPHYLUM: Crustacea

TYPE: Invertebrates

INCLUDES: Crabs, Lobsters, Shrimp, Barnacles

BODY COVERING: Hard exoskeleton

HABITAT: Mostly aquatic (oceans freshwater)

LEGS: Often 10 legs (varies by species)
