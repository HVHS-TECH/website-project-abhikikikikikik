All About Whales

The Blue Whale is one of the most recognizable sea animals, holding the title for the largest animal to ever live on Earth, even bigger than the largest dinosaurs! These massive mammals can grow to about 30 meters long, give birth to baby whales weighing 2 to 3 tonnes, and have tongues weighing as much as an elephant. Despite their huge size, they feast on tiny little shrimp called krill. Since they are mammals, they must swim to the ocean's surface to breathe air through their blowholes before diving back into the deep.

GROUP NAME: Whales (Cetaceans)

SCIENTIFIC INFRAORDER: Cetacea

TYPE: Marine Mammals

SUB-GROUPS: Baleen Whales and Toothed Whales

BREATHING: Lungs (must surface for air through blowholes)

HABITAT: All of the world's oceans

KEY FEATURE: High intelligence and complex song communication
