All About Cetaceans

Cetaceans are a group of highly intelligent marine mammals that includes whales, dolphins, and porpoises. Although they live entirely in water, they are not considered 'fish' since they breathe air through blowholes, are warm-blooded, and give birth to live young. Ancestors of cetaceans were actually four-legged land animals that moved into the ocean millions of years ago. Over time, their front legs evolved into flippers and their back legs disappeared, allowing them to become true masters of the deep sea.

GROUP NAME: Cetaceans

SCIENTIFIC ORDER: Cetacea

TYPE: Marine Mammals

INCLUDES: Whales, Dolphins, Porpoises

HABITAT: Oceans worldwide

BREATHING: Air through blowholes

SPECIAL ABILITY: Echolocation (in toothed species)
