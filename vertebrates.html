Understanding Vertebrates

Vertebrates are a wide group of animals that all share one defining feature: a backbone or spinal column. This internal skeleton provides a strong framework that strengthens the body and protects the highly developed nervous system from damage. From the smallest fish in the sea to the largest mammals on land, vertebrates have evolved to conquer almost every habitat on Earth.

The Core Classes

Scientists generally divide vertebrates into five main groups: Fish, Amphibians, Reptiles, Birds, and Mammals. While they all look very different and unique, they share complicated features like centralized brain and complex organ systems. These special traits have allowed them to become some of, if not the most intelligent and physically capable creatures in the entire animal kingdom! Primates represent this mental and physical capability very well, as some of them have the IQ of a small child, and can solve a list of logic puzzles. Primates are known to use tools for finding food and self care, and are some of the only animals that recognize themselves when shown in a mirror.
