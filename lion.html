All About Lions

The African Lion is one of the most powerful apex predators in the world. They stand out among other big cats because they are highly social, living in family groups called prides. While the males are highly known for their thick, majestic manes that protect their necks during fights, the females are the ones hunting for the group. These "Kings of the Jungle" normally spend most of their day resting, with resting periods being up to 20 hours long save their energy for high-speed chases across the savanna.

SPECIES: African Lion

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Panthera leo

GROUP NAME: A Pride

DIET: Carnivore (Apex Predator)

HABITAT: Grasslands, Savannas, and Open Woodlands

TOP SPEED: Up to 80 km/h in short bursts

KEY FEATURE: Social behavior and the male's impressive mane
