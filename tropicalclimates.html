All About Tropical Climates

Tropical climates are found near the Earth's equator and are famous for having warm weather, tropical beaches, and turquoise waters. Since tropical climates take in plenty of rainfall and sunlight, they're usually home to the most diverse range of life on the planet. From the colossal trees of the Amazon rainforest to the bright coral reefs in the ocean, these tropical climates are home to many colorful birds, primates, amphibians, insects, and reptiles. The consistent warmth means animals here don't need to hibernate, but instead have to compete for space in their crowded environments.

REGION TYPE: Tropical Climate (Rainforest / Savanna)

AVERAGE TEMP: Always warm (usually above 18°C)

PRECIPITATION: High (Heavy rainfall throughout the year)

SEASONS: Wet and Dry (No winter or summer)

BIODIVERSITY: Home to over 50% of the world's species

KEY FEATURE: Dense vegetation and rapid nutrient cycling

LOCATIONS: Amazon Basin, Congo, Southeast Asia
