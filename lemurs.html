All About Lemurs

Most animals hibernate during cold seasons. However, the Fat-tailed Dwarf Lemur of Madagascar hibernates to survive the dry season when food and water are scarce, being the only primates known to hibernate for long periods. Before settling in for hibernation, they store extra fat in their tails, which can double in size, giving them the name 'Fat-tailed lemur'. By slowing down their metabolism and sleeping for up to seven months, they can survive in a tropical environment where other animals might struggle to find enough to eat.

SPECIES: Fat-tailed Dwarf Lemur

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Cheirogaleus medius

HIBERNATION TYPE: Estivation (Dry season sleep)

DURATION: Up to 7 months

ENERGY SOURCE: Fat stored in their tails

HABITAT: Tropical dry forests of Madagascar

KEY FACT: They are the only primates known to hibernate for long periods
