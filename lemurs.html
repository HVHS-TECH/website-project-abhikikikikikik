All About Lemurs

Most animals hibernate during cold season. However, the Fat-tailed Dwarf Lemur of Madagascar hibernates to survive hot seasons, since they are the only primates known by scientists to hibernate for long periods. Before they actually start to hibernate , they store fat in their tails, which doubles the size of their tails. This gives them the name 'Fat-tailed lemur', and by slowing down their metabolism, they can survive in a tropical environment where other animals might struggle to find enough to eat.

SPECIES: Fat-tailed Dwarf Lemur

SCIENTIFIC NAME: Cheirogaleus medius

HIBERNATION TYPE: Estivation (Dry season sleep)

DURATION: Up to 7 months

ENERGY SOURCE: Fat stored in their tails

HABITAT: Tropical dry forests of Madagascar

KEY FACT: They are the only primates known to hibernate for long periods
