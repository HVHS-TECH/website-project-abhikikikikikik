All About Mammals

Mammals are an incredible group of animals that can be found in almost every corner of the globe. They can be found in the freezing polar ice caps of Antarticta, and the deepest parts of the ocean. What seperates and differentiates them from other animals are the fact that they're warm blooded, have hair or fur at some point in their lives, they don't lay eggs, and they possess a unique ability to create milk for their young.
