All About Mammals

Mammals are a wide group of animals that can be found in almost every corner of the globe. They can be found in the freezing polar ice caps of Antarctica, and the deepest parts of the ocean. What separates and differentiates them from other animals are the fact that they're warm blooded, have hair or fur at some point in their lives, they don't lay eggs, and they possess a unique ability to create milk for their young.

The Three Mammal Groups

Scientists usually divide mammals into three unique groups based on how they develop. Placentals, like lions and humans, grow inside until they are ready to be born. Marsupials, such as kangaroos, have pouches and give birth to very small animals while inside the pouch. Finally, Monotremes are some of the strangest mammals, like the platypus, since they lay eggs!
